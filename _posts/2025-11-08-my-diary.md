## **2. Save/Export Your Note**

- In Obsidian, select all (Ctrl+A) and copy your note (including frontmatter).
    
- Open Notepad or any online text editor, paste, and save as a `.md` file (e.g., `2025-11-08-my-diary.md`).
    

## **3. Upload Directly to GitHub Repository (Browser Only)**

- Go to your forked/fresh repo of the Gaohaoyang theme at [https://github.com/Gaohaoyang/gaohaoyang.github.io](https://github.com/Gaohaoyang/gaohaoyang.github.io) (or your own fork).
    
- **Click into the `_posts` folder.**
    
- Use **“Add file” > “Upload files”** to drag and drop your new `.md` diary entry.
    
- For images:
    
    - Upload images to the relevant `/images` or `/assets` folder.
        
    - Reference them in your post with standard Markdown:
        
        text
        
        `![My note photo](/images/your-image.png)`
        
- **Commit changes**—your site will update automatically.
    

## **4. Your Diary is Online!**

- GitHub Pages will publish your diary at `https://yourusername.github.io`.
    
- New entries can be added by repeating steps 1–3.
    

---

## **Features & Benefits**

- **NO local Jekyll/Ruby/Git install required**—100% browser-based after Obsidian.
    
- Images and markdown notes stored for free, instantly live whenever you upload.
    
- Beautiful theme and organizational features (tags, categories) fully supported.
    
- **Everything backed up and future-proofed in your GitHub repo.**
